CASE FILE / CIVILIAN HARM · REVIEWED 18 OCT 2026

The Engineers’ Building strike

A source-led case file on Human Rights Watch’s investigation into a strike on the six-storey Engineers’ Building, including the organisation’s identified casualty figures and review of visual material.

INCIDENT / 31 October 2023LOCATION / Central Gaza StripSTATUS / SOURCE-ATTRIBUTED

Status note: Human Rights Watch investigation · legal assessment attributed to HRW. NOOR is a source index, not a court, investigative authority or real-time verification service.

01 / SCOPE

What the linked record supports—and where it stops.

Human Rights Watch identified at least 106 civilians killed, including 54 children. The organisation said it reviewed satellite imagery, 35 photographs and 45 videos, interviewed witnesses and found no evidence of a military target in the vicinity. HRW described the strike as an apparent war crime. These are HRW’s reported findings and legal assessment—not a judicial ruling. “No evidence found” describes the scope of a specific investigation; it is not a claim that this archive has independently examined every possible source.

Read the original sources below before quoting a number, legal characterization or disputed account. This page summarizes and attributes; it does not independently re-investigate the incident.
