CASE FILE / DESTRUCTION · REVIEWED 18 OCT 2026

Destruction of Gaza City high-rise buildings

A source-linked overview of Amnesty International’s investigation into the destruction or severe damage of residential and commercial multi-storey buildings in Gaza City.

INCIDENT / September–October 2025LOCATION / Gaza CitySTATUS / SOURCE-ATTRIBUTED

Status note: Amnesty International investigation · legal characterization attributed to Amnesty. NOOR is a source index, not a court, investigative authority or real-time verification service.

01 / SCOPE

What the linked record supports—and where it stops.

Amnesty International reported that it examined at least 13 multi-storey buildings destroyed or severely damaged in September and October 2025, interviewing former residents and witnesses and analysing satellite imagery and more than 25 videos. Amnesty called for the destruction to be investigated as potential war crimes. That is the organisation’s assessment and recommendation, not a court judgment. This page links the investigation and the official statement cited within it without independently deciding the incident-specific legal questions.

Read the original sources below before quoting a number, legal characterization or disputed account. This page summarizes and attributes; it does not independently re-investigate the incident.
