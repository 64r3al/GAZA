02 / PROVENANCE

Why this index avoids re-uploading footage.

Keeping a link at the originating publisher preserves the uploader, context, corrections, date and moderation notes. This catalogue does not guarantee the authenticity of each item; it explains what a named publisher says it examined and points readers to that method. Source availability may change.
