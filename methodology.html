SOURCE STANDARDS / EDITORIAL METHOD

A record should show its work.

NOOR organizes publicly available reporting, documentary material, witness accounts and legal records into a navigable research archive. The purpose is to make the source trail easier to follow—not to replace the original investigators, courts, journalists or humanitarian agencies.

01 / INCLUSION

What belongs in the archive?

Records are selected because they add a traceable public source about an incident, humanitarian condition, documented personal account, visual record, investigation or legal proceeding relevant to civilian life in Gaza and the occupied Palestinian territory. Priority is given to primary documents and named, accountable publishers with a stated method.

A record is not included merely because it is viral, emotionally compelling or consistent with an existing view. Social posts may be included when they are relevant primary material, official statements or a trail to further reporting; their origin and limitations must remain visible.

A / SOURCE

Traceable origin

Link directly to the report, document, video or original post where possible. Secondary coverage is labelled as reporting, not presented as the underlying source.

B / SCOPE

Clear limits

Each case should state what its linked source supports and what it does not establish. Missing details and conflicting accounts must not be filled with guesses.

02 / VERIFICATION

A link is not the same as independent verification.

NOOR is a curator of public records, not an on-the-ground investigative team. Unless a record explicitly documents an independent verification process, the archive attributes claims to the source that made them. When accounts conflict, the record should preserve the disagreement and link each available account rather than quietly choose one.

01 / PRIMARY MATERIAL

Original footage and documents

The uploader, publisher, date and stated context are recorded where available. A clip can show what appears in the frame; without corroboration it may not establish exact date, location, identity or responsibility.

02 / SECONDARY REPORTING

Investigations and journalism

Records identify the publisher and link to its findings. Methodology, limitations, sourcing and any published response from parties named in the report should be considered alongside its conclusions.

If NOOR cannot independently validate a material detail, it should say so. Confidence is not a substitute for corroboration, and a source link is not a guarantee that every claim in the source is accurate.

03 / LABELS

Read each status carefully.

Status labels describe the kind of material or procedural step the source reports. They are not decorative trust badges and do not replace reading the linked source.

Reported figures

A named organization or authority published figures for a specified reporting window. Figures can be revised and may not represent a complete case-by-case register.

Investigation or NGO finding

An investigative organization published conclusions under its own methodology. Attribute the finding to that organization and review its evidence, scope and response notes.

Official or primary-source material

A statement, post, document or recording originates with the named institution or uploader. Authenticity and context still require care; a party's statement is evidence of what it said, not automatic proof of every claim.

Contested or unverified detail

Available public material does not resolve a detail, sources disagree, or the record has not established the specific fact. Do not treat an absence of verification as proof of either side's account.

04 / CHRONOLOGY

Event dates and publication dates are different.

The incident date or reporting window belongs to the event being described. The publication date belongs to the article, statement or document. A source may be published later, updated after publication, or cover a period rather than a single moment. If the event date is unclear, the record should say that instead of presenting a guessed date as certain.

05 / LEGAL STATUS

Allegations, proceedings and judgments are not interchangeable.

A report that an investigation has opened is not a finding. An arrest warrant is not a conviction. A provisional or jurisdictional order is not necessarily a final ruling on the merits. Records about the International Criminal Court, the International Court of Justice, UN bodies or national courts must identify the institution, procedural step, date and exact scope of the decision using the official document wherever available.

NOOR does not decide criminal liability, offer legal advice or declare a person guilty. Legal conclusions are attributed to the institution that made them and shown with their actual procedural status.

06 / VISUAL MATERIAL

Distressing material must keep its context.

Where imagery or footage may be distressing, the surrounding page should state that clearly before media is loaded. Embeds should require a deliberate action where practical, never autoplay sound, and preserve a route to the original publisher. Captions should identify the source, publication date and any licence or attribution requirement.

An 18+ confirmation is an access warning, not proof of age and not a replacement for content warnings, respectful presentation or source verification. Graphic detail is never a substitute for the information needed to understand an incident.

07 / CORRECTIONS

Corrections are part of the record.

Source pages can change, links can expire and new evidence may alter the understanding of an event. If you identify a broken source, a factual mistake, an incorrect attribution, a licence issue or material contrary evidence, send a specific correction with the relevant URL and supporting documentation through the project's public issue tracker.

The archive is curated, not exhaustive. Some events lack sufficient accessible documentation to be included; absence from the archive does not imply that an event did not happen. The project's selections and summaries may be incomplete and should be read alongside the linked original material.
