Hind Rajab and the rescue response

A carefully scoped timeline of published reconstruction work and reporting about five-year-old Hind Rajab, the emergency call for help and the ambulance crew sent to reach her.

INCIDENT / 29 January 2024LOCATION / Gaza CitySTATUS / SOURCE-ATTRIBUTED

Status note: Reconstruction and reported military investigation · no verdict stated. NOOR is a source index, not a court, investigative authority or real-time verification service.

01 / SCOPE

What the linked record supports—and where it stops.

The archive record draws on a Reuters report published on 19 August 2026, a Washington Post interactive reconstruction, Forensic Architecture’s analysis and WAFA’s report on audio released by the Palestine Red Crescent Society. Reuters reported that the Israeli military acknowledged troops opened fire on the car and said it had opened a criminal investigation. The reporting and reconstructions are attributed to their publishers; this file does not substitute for investigative records or a court finding.

Read the original sources below before quoting a number, legal characterization or disputed account. This page summarizes and attributes; it does not independently re-investigate the incident.

02 / TIMELINE

A chronology, not a verdict.

Event dates and publication dates are separated. Each entry describes what a named source reported or published.

  1. 29 JAN 2024

    Emergency call and rescue attempt

    Published accounts describe Hind Rajab being trapped in a car with relatives who had been killed and calling emergency dispatchers for help. An ambulance sent to reach her was later found destroyed, and two paramedics were killed.

  2. 2024

    Reconstruction work published

    The Washington Post and Forensic Architecture published reconstructions drawing on available audio, location and other material. Each reconstruction has its own method and limits.

  3. 19 AUG 2026

    Reuters reports an announced probe

    Reuters reports that Israel’s military acknowledged troops opened fire on the car and said it had opened a criminal investigation. An announced investigation is not a verdict.
