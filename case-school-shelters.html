CASE FILE / SCHOOLS & SHELTERS · REVIEWED 18 OCT 2026

Attacks on schools used as shelters

A comparative case file about Human Rights Watch’s investigation of strikes on Khadija girls’ school and al-Zeitoun C school while used as shelters.

INCIDENT / 27 July and 21 September 2024LOCATION / Deir al-Balah and Gaza CitySTATUS / SOURCE-ATTRIBUTED

Status note: Human Rights Watch investigation · findings attributed to HRW. NOOR is a source index, not a court, investigative authority or real-time verification service.

01 / SCOPE

What the linked record supports—and where it stops.

HRW investigated a strike on Khadija girls’ school in Deir al-Balah on 27 July 2024 and al-Zeitoun C school in Gaza City on 21 September 2024. Its report said at least 15 people were killed in the first and at least 34 in the second, and reported no evidence of a military target at either school. The casualty figures and assessment are HRW’s findings based on witness accounts and visual evidence, not a court judgment. The two events remain separate entries in the chronology.

Read the original sources below before quoting a number, legal characterization or disputed account. This page summarizes and attributes; it does not independently re-investigate the incident.
