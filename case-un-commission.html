The UN Commission report under the Genocide Convention

A source-led guide to the Commission of Inquiry’s report published on 16 September 2025, the Commission’s stated findings, the government response, and the distinction between an inquiry and a court case.

PUBLICATION / 16 SEP 2025BODY / UN COMMISSION OF INQUIRYSTATUS / FORMAL INQUIRY FINDING

Status note: the findings below are attributed to the Commission. They are not an International Criminal Court conviction and are not the final merits judgment in the separate International Court of Justice case.

01 / WHAT WAS PUBLISHED

Read the report as the Commission’s own finding.

On 16 September 2025, the UN Independent International Commission of Inquiry published a conference room paper examining conduct in Gaza under the Genocide Convention. The Commission concluded that Israeli authorities and security forces had committed four of the five acts defined in the Convention and found reasonable grounds for the required specific intent.

The Government of Israel strongly rejected the report and challenged its mandate, methods and conclusions. Both the original report and the government response are linked here so readers can assess the language and reasoning directly. This file attributes each position to its issuer; it does not present a summary as a substitute for the complete documents.
