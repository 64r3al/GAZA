READING THE RECORD / 00

A source trail, not a verdict machine.

This is a curated index, not an exhaustive database or a court register. The status label describes what the linked reporting says was published, verified, acknowledged or announced. It does not imply that NOOR independently proved the allegations or that a legal case has reached judgment. Humanitarian updates are kept distinct from incident investigations.

Dates describe the incident or reporting window where known; publication dates are shown separately. “Footage linked” means the source contains or links visual/audio material—it is not an endorsement of authenticity beyond the source's own stated method. This archive does not embed graphic footage or autoplay audio.

RECORD / 001UN-reported figures

Children killed and injured despite the ceasefire

UNICEF reported that more than 340 children had been reported killed since the ceasefire was announced. In the two weeks covered by its 16 October briefing, seven children were reported killed, two boys later died of earlier injuries, and at least 33 children were seriously injured. These are reported figures, not a complete case-by-case register.

RECORD / 005Investigation published

Destruction of residential and commercial high-rise buildings

Amnesty International's May 2026 investigation examined at least 13 multi-storey buildings destroyed or severely damaged in Gaza City during September and October 2025. It interviewed former residents and witnesses and analysed satellite imagery and more than 25 videos. Amnesty called for the destruction to be investigated as potential war crimes; that is the organisation's assessment, not a court judgment.

RECORD / 009NGO investigation

Attacks on schools used as shelters

Human Rights Watch investigated two strikes: Khadija girls' school in Deir al-Balah on 27 July 2024 and al-Zeitoun C school in Gaza City on 21 September 2024. HRW said at least 15 people were killed in the first strike and at least 34 in the second, and reported no evidence of a military target at either school. These are the organisation's findings based on witness accounts and visual evidence, not a court judgment.

RECORD / 010Investigation reported

Hind Rajab and the rescue response

Five-year-old Hind Rajab was trapped in a car in Gaza City on 29 January 2024 with relatives who had been killed. She called emergency dispatchers for help; the ambulance sent to reach her was later found destroyed, and two paramedics were killed. Reuters reported on 19 August 2026 that Israel's military acknowledged its troops opened fire on the car and said it had opened a criminal investigation. An investigation is not a verdict.

STANDARD / 03

What belongs in this archive?

Each entry must have a date or stated reporting window, a location as specific as the source supports, a named source, and a clear distinction between reporting, investigation, allegation and legal judgment. When a source corrects a figure or its status changes, update the record with the date and a link to that change rather than silently overwriting its history.

NOOR is an independent awareness project. It is not a court, newsroom, investigative body or humanitarian agency, and it does not collect donations. Please report broken links or source corrections through the project's repository.
