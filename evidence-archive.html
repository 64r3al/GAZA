READING THE RECORD / 00

A source trail, not a verdict machine.

This is a curated index, not an exhaustive database or a court register. The status label describes what the linked reporting says was published, verified, acknowledged or announced. It does not imply that NOOR independently proved the allegations or that a legal case has reached judgment. Humanitarian updates are kept distinct from incident investigations.

Dates describe the incident or reporting window where known; publication dates are shown separately. “Footage linked” means the source contains or links visual/audio material—it is not an endorsement of authenticity beyond the source's own stated method. This archive does not embed graphic footage or autoplay audio.

RECORD / 011Human-rights investigation

One residential strike killed 106 civilians, including 54 children

Human Rights Watch investigated an Israeli airstrike on the six-storey Engineers’ Building in central Gaza on 31 October 2023. HRW identified at least 106 civilians killed, including 54 children; witnesses said 350 or more people were sheltering there, including families displaced from elsewhere. Researchers reviewed satellite imagery, 35 photographs and 45 videos of the aftermath. HRW found no evidence of a military target in the vicinity and described the strike as an apparent war crime. This is HRW’s assessment, not a court verdict.

RECORD / 012UN commission finding

UN Commission of Inquiry’s genocide finding — and Israel’s rejection

On 16 September 2025, the UN Independent International Commission of Inquiry concluded that Israeli authorities and security forces had committed four of the five underlying acts defined in the Genocide Convention in Gaza, and found reasonable grounds for the required specific intent. Israel strongly rejected the report and challenged the Commission’s mandate, methods and conclusions. This is a commission’s formal finding, not a final criminal conviction or a final judgment by the International Court of Justice; the ICJ case remains a separate proceeding.

Scope

Formal UN commission finding; not a final court judgment
Footage status
Report analyses evidence and official statements; links only
Sources and scope notes

The Commission cited killings, serious bodily or mental harm, destructive conditions of life and measures intended to prevent births. Israel rejects the finding. The International Court of Justice genocide case has not reached a final merits judgment as of this archive update; readers should distinguish a commission report, the parties’ positions and a court judgment.

RECORD / 014International court warrants

ICC arrest warrants over alleged crimes against civilians in Gaza

On 21 November 2024, the International Criminal Court issued arrest warrants for Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and former Defence Minister Yoav Gallant. The Pre-Trial Chamber found reasonable grounds to believe they bore criminal responsibility for the alleged war crime of starvation as a method of warfare, alleged attacks on civilians, and alleged crimes against humanity including murder, persecution and other inhumane acts. The warrants are not convictions; Israel challenged the Court’s jurisdiction, and the Court rejected its challenges to the warrants at that stage.

Conduct period
8 Oct 2023 – 20 May 2024
Jurisdiction
International Criminal Court · Palestine situation
Status / legal note
Arrest warrants based on reasonable grounds; no conviction
Footage status
Court documents and legal materials linked
Sources and scope notes

An arrest warrant means judges found reasonable grounds at the warrant stage; it is not a final verdict after trial. The ICC’s findings concern specific individuals and alleged conduct during the stated period, not automatic legal guilt of an entire population or every member of the armed forces.

STANDARD / 03

What belongs in this archive?

Each entry must have a date or stated reporting window, a location as specific as the source supports, a named source, and a clear distinction between reporting, investigation, allegation and legal judgment. When a source corrects a figure or its status changes, update the record with the date and a link to that change rather than silently overwriting its history.

NOOR is an independent awareness project. It is not a court, newsroom, investigative body or humanitarian agency, and it does not collect donations. Please report broken links or source corrections through the project's repository.
