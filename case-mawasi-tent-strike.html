The Mawasi tent strike

A narrowly scoped file on Reuters’ report that a tent was struck in the Mawasi area late on 11 October 2026, with the source’s stated casualty count and the military’s account kept distinct.

INCIDENT / 11 October 2026LOCATION / Mawasi area, Khan YounisSTATUS / SOURCE-ATTRIBUTED

Status note: News reporting with competing accounts · legal responsibility not determined here. NOOR is a source index, not a court, investigative authority or real-time verification service.

01 / SCOPE

What the linked record supports—and where it stops.

Reuters reported on 12 October that a strike late on 11 October hit a tent in the Mawasi area of Khan Younis and killed three people, according to local medics. The Israeli military said it was targeting militants in the area. Reuters also described a separate strike on 12 October; this file is limited to the tent strike described for 11 October. The archive does not independently resolve the competing accounts, verify every casualty detail or determine legal responsibility.

Read the original sources below before quoting a number, legal characterization or disputed account. This page summarizes and attributes; it does not independently re-investigate the incident.
