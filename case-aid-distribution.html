CASE FILE / AID ACCESS · REVIEWED 18 OCT 2026

Shootings near food distribution sites

A source-led record of reporting about mass-casualty incidents near Gaza Humanitarian Foundation aid sites and deaths reported while people were seeking food.

INCIDENT / 27 May–31 July 2025LOCATION / Multiple food-distribution locations in GazaSTATUS / SOURCE-ATTRIBUTED

Status note: Human Rights Watch report · cited casualty figures and legal assessment attributed. NOOR is a source index, not a court, investigative authority or real-time verification service.

01 / SCOPE

What the linked record supports—and where it stops.

Human Rights Watch published a report on 1 August 2025 citing UN figures of at least 859 Palestinians killed while seeking aid between 27 May and 31 July 2025 and characterizing the conduct as war crimes. The number is cited by HRW for that period; it is not a live total calculated by NOOR. HRW’s legal characterization is not a judgment issued by a court. This record does not collapse separate incidents into one event or infer individual responsibility.

Read the original sources below before quoting a number, legal characterization or disputed account. This page summarizes and attributes; it does not independently re-investigate the incident.
