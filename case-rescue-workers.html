CASE FILE / AID WORKERS · REVIEWED 18 OCT 2026

The Tal as-Sultan rescue convoy

A linked chronology of the deaths of emergency workers near Tal as-Sultan and subsequent analysis of phone footage, audio and reporting about an announced investigation.

INCIDENT / 23 March 2025LOCATION / Tal as-Sultan, RafahSTATUS / SOURCE-ATTRIBUTED

Status note: Visual analysis and later reported investigation · no verdict stated. NOOR is a source index, not a court, investigative authority or real-time verification service.

01 / SCOPE

What the linked record supports—and where it stops.

Bellingcat’s analysis uses phone video recovered from one of the dead workers and describes geolocation and audio review. A Palestine Red Crescent Society social post is linked as original material, and Reuters later reported an Israeli investigation into the deaths of 15 emergency workers. This file distinguishes evidence analysis, original social material and later news reporting. It does not claim that every disputed detail has been resolved or that a court has issued a verdict.

Read the original sources below before quoting a number, legal characterization or disputed account. This page summarizes and attributes; it does not independently re-investigate the incident.

02 / TIMELINE

A chronology, not a verdict.

Event dates and publication dates are separated. Each entry describes what a named source reported or published.

  1. 23 MAR 2025

    Loss of contact

    The archive records that a convoy of Palestine Red Crescent Society, Civil Defense and UN aid workers lost contact near Tal as-Sultan.

  2. 9 APR 2025

    Bellingcat publishes analysis

    Bellingcat describes geolocation and audio analysis of phone footage recovered from one of the workers who died.

  3. 19 AUG 2026

    Reuters reports investigation

    Reuters reports that Israel said it would investigate the deaths of 15 emergency workers. The existence of an investigation does not establish its conclusions.
